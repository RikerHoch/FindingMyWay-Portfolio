> 🌱 **Finding My Way** is a personal learning journey focused on developing transferable skills, strengthening professional practice, and reflecting on my growth as a learner in a technological context.

## 📖 About the Portfolio

This portfolio is the summative assessment for **TS7501 at Whitecliffe**. It brings together selected learning experiences, projects, collaborative activities, and supporting evidence to demonstrate my individual development over time.

Through reflection and practical examples, I explore how I apply transferable skills to my work, communicate and collaborate with others, and identify opportunities to improve my approach to learning and professional development.

The portfolio emphasises not only what I have achieved, but also **how I work, what I have learned, and how I intend to grow**.

## 🎯 Learning Outcomes

This portfolio addresses the following learning outcomes:

- **LO1 — Transferable Skills:** Apply transferable skills and attributes to identify better ways of working in creative or technological contexts.
- **LO2 — Collaboration & Communication:** Use collaboration and communication strategies and tools to enhance outcomes in a chosen activity.
- **LO3 — Reflection & Development:** Reflect on personal development to identify improvements in learning and professional practice.

## 📂 Portfolio Structure

Each project or activity is presented as an individual entry, following a consistent reflective approach.

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Context** | Introduces the activity, project, or learning experience. |
| **Transferable Skills** | Identifies the skills relevant to the experience. |
| **Application & Analysis** | Explains how I applied those skills, including strategies and tools used. |
| **Reflection & Insight** | Examines the outcome, what I learned, and what I would improve. |
| **Future Action** | Identifies practical steps for applying the learning in future work. |
| **Supporting Evidence** | Links relevant artefacts, screenshots, notes, or project outputs to the reflection. |

## 🧠 Reflective Approach

My reflections use four key stages:

1. **Description** — What happened, and what was my role?
2. **Analysis** — What skills, strategies, or tools did I use? What worked or could have been improved?
3. **Insight** — What did the experience teach me about my approach?
4. **Future Action** — How will I apply this learning to future projects and professional practice?

This structure helps connect practical experiences with meaningful learning and improvement.
